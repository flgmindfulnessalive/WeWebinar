An **evergreen webinar** is a presentation you record once, then replay as if it were live -- at the times you set, with chat, polls, and calls to action (CTAs) triggered at exact moments in the video.

The difference from just "uploading a video" is that viewers can't skip ahead or rewind: they experience the session the same way they'd experience a real live webinar, with the sense that it's happening right now.

## Why automate your webinar

- **Scales without your time.** You record once and the presentation runs every day, without you needing to show up.
- **Keeps live-level conversion.** Simulated chat, reactions, and timed CTAs recreate the urgency and interaction that make live webinars convert better than an on-demand video.
- **Frees you to sell, not present.** The time you used to spend repeating the same talk can go into lead follow-up, product, or support instead.

## How it works in practice

1. Record your presentation once (or upload one you already have).
2. Set the times it's available: fixed slots or "just-in-time" (starts within the next few minutes, whenever the visitor shows up).
3. Schedule the simulated chat, frequently asked questions, and CTAs to appear at the exact point in the video they belong.
4. Each registrant experiences their own server-synced session, so they can't skip ahead or jump to the end.

A well-built evergreen webinar doesn't replace the connection of a real live webinar -- it complements it, giving you a way to sell consistently every day of the week.
