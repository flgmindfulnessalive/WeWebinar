Before you build your next presentation, there's a decision that shapes everything else: are you going to deliver it live, or automate it? It's not a question of which tool to use — it's a question of what your business needs right now.

## What a live webinar is

A live webinar is exactly what it sounds like: you connect at a fixed time, on a platform like Zoom, Google Meet, or YouTube Live, and present in real time to whoever registered. You can answer questions instantly, read the room, and adjust your presentation on the fly.

It's the format with the most human connection — and also the one that depends the most on you.

## What an automated (evergreen) webinar is

An [evergreen webinar](/blog/what-is-an-evergreen-webinar) is a recording that plays back as if it were live: scheduled times, simulated chat, polls, and calls to action synced to the video. The viewer can't skip ahead or jump around — they experience the session in real time, even though you only recorded it once.

## The differences that actually matter

| | Live webinar | Automated webinar |
|---|---|---|
| Your time | An hour for every session you give | Recorded once, runs indefinitely |
| Time zone | A fixed time excludes half the world | Adapts to each visitor |
| Consistency | Energy and message vary session to session | The same presentation, polished, every time |
| Interaction | Real questions, in the moment | Simulated chat + your availability to answer by email or support |
| Scale | Limited by how many times you can repeat the talk | Unlimited |

No column is "better" in the abstract. It depends on what you're selling and what stage your business is at.

## When a live webinar makes sense

- You're launching something new and need to validate the message in real time by reading the audience's reactions.
- You sell something high-ticket where trust is built by answering hard questions on the spot.
- Your audience is small and known — a group of existing customers, a closed community.

## When it makes sense to automate it

- You've already validated your presentation (you've given it live a few times and it works) and now you want it to work without you.
- Your audience is spread across multiple time zones.
- You need the webinar available every day, not just when you can be there.
- Your business's bottleneck is your own time, not the quality of the presentation.

## The most common answer: both, at different moments

In practice, most businesses that sell with webinars don't pick one format forever — they use live to test and refine the message, and once the presentation converts consistently, they record it and turn it into an evergreen asset. Live validates; evergreen scales.

If you already have a presentation that works and you're still repeating it by hand every week, that's exactly the moment to automate it.
